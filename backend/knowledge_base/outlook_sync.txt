Outlook Email Sync Troubleshooting

Symptoms:
- Outlook is not receiving new emails.
- The inbox is not synchronizing.
- Outlook displays a disconnected status.

Troubleshooting:
1. Check that the device has an active internet connection.
2. Check whether Outlook displays Working Offline or Disconnected.
3. If Working Offline is enabled, disable it and allow Outlook to reconnect.
4. Restart Outlook and check whether new emails arrive.
5. If synchronization still fails, contact IT Support.

Security:
- Never share passwords or MFA codes.
- Do not delete mail profiles or data without IT approval.

Escalation:
If email synchronization continues to fail, contact IT Support with the error message.
