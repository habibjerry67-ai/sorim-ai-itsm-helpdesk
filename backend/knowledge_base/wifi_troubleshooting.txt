Corporate Wi-Fi Troubleshooting

If an employee cannot connect to corporate Wi-Fi, verify that Wi-Fi is enabled and that the employee has selected the correct corporate network.

Ask the employee to disconnect and reconnect. Check whether other devices can connect and whether the issue affects one employee or multiple employees.

If the device still cannot connect, record the error message and device details. Escalate authentication failures to Network Support and widespread connectivity failures to the network operations team.

Do not ask employees to share their passwords. Record troubleshooting actions and results in the support ticket.
