# VPN Troubleshooting

Symptoms:
- VPN fails to connect.
- Employee cannot access internal company resources.
- VPN connection repeatedly disconnects.

Troubleshooting:
1. Check that the device has an active internet connection.
2. Confirm that the approved company VPN client is installed and updated.
3. Disconnect and reconnect using the company's approved VPN configuration.
4. If the issue continues, contact Network Support with the error message and approximate time of failure.

Security:
- Never share passwords or MFA codes with support staff.
- Do not disable security controls or change firewall settings without IT authorization.

Escalation:
If multiple employees are affected or the VPN outage blocks critical work, escalate to Network Support.
