Password Expiry and Account Recovery

If an employee cannot log in because their password has expired, verify the reported issue and direct them to the organization's approved password-reset process.

If the account is locked, Identity Support should verify the employee's identity and follow the account-unlock procedure. Multi-factor authentication should be used where required.

For a mock automated password reset, record the employee username, action performed, result, and timestamp in the audit log. Validate the simulated result before reporting success.

Never store plaintext passwords or ask employees to share passwords, recovery codes, or MFA codes. Escalate repeated failures to Identity Support.
